Are the mentoring sessions mandatory?

No. Mentoring sessions take place online on Fridays from 13:00 to 15:00.

If they conflict with your work schedule, you can comfortably work at your own pace. All videos, exercises, and reading assignments for each module are available on Omniway and on this course portal. What must be submitted on time are the two formal assessments (see the next question).

How do I join the mentoring sessions?

Mentoring sessions are held on Fridays at 13:00–15:00 via Microsoft Teams. The link is posted in Omniway under Teaching, in each week's mentoring module. A new link is generated for each session, so please grab it from that week's block.

What is required to pass the course?

The course has two formal examination milestones, and both must be submitted and awarded a passing grade:

  • Knowledge Assessment A: An individual written submission, due in Module 13.
  • Knowledge Assessment B: An individual practical software project: an AI-assisted development workflow that you plan, execute, present, and submit in Modules 14–20.

Grades awarded are IG (Fail), G (Pass), and VG (Pass with Distinction). For each assessment, there is an initial deadline plus two opportunities for retakes or supplementary submissions.

Weekly exercises are not individually graded, but they directly train the exact competencies evaluated in the assessments.

How is the course structured?

The course consists of 20 weekly modules based on a flipped classroom pedagogical model. In each module:

  1. Watch the module's instructional video.
  2. Complete the exercises in sequence: initial tasks build foundational skills; latter exercises provide advanced depth.
  3. Read the assigned texts in the Reading Guide for that module.
  4. Bring your completed work and any questions to Friday's mentoring workshop.

Not all modules have their own standalone video or exercise sheet; Omniway outlines the specific focus for those weeks.

Where can I find the course materials?

  • In Omniway: Under Teaching, each module block includes direct links to that week's materials.
  • Here on the course site: Everything is consolidated in one place: Installation, Videos, Exercises, Reading Guide, and Glossary.

Links within Omniway point directly to these pages, so all content remains synchronized.

What do I need to install, and does it cost anything?

The full setup is detailed in the Installation Guide for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Plan for approximately 30–45 minutes. Complete this setup before your first mentoring session.

The tools and software are free to use, though several require creating accounts. One tool requires a paid subscription tier, while for another tool students can apply for a complimentary educational license. Full instructions are in the Before You Begin – Three Accounts section of the guide.

If you encounter setup blockers: take a screenshot of the terminal error and message the instructor through Omniway.

Do I need prior programming knowledge?

For course exercises, you can choose your preferred programming language: Python, C#, or Java. Code examples throughout the curriculum are primarily in Python.

If you are looking to learn programming or refresh your fundamentals, an optional self-paced Python track is available in Omniway with short video tutorials in English that build sequentially.

What language are the videos in?

The main course lectures are in Swedish. Videos in the optional introductory Python track are in English.

How do I ask questions?

Send a direct message to the instructor via Omniway. You are also encouraged to bring questions to the Friday mentoring sessions so fellow students can benefit from the discussion.

If troubleshooting an exercise error or broken CLI command: specify the module number, exercise number, and attach a clear screenshot.
